### Moscow · 3024

Olga finds the red machine beside the wall. She unlocks it, steps inside, and presses the blue button. The drawer opens, just as Alex said it would. Inside is a recorder no larger than her phone.

When she lifts it, the screen shows a photograph of Alex in a gray workroom. Behind him are tall windows and strange computers. There is also a picture of a little boy with his mother. The boy has Alex's eyes.

Olga sits down and opens the notebook. She wants to understand the life behind his story. On one page, she finds the instructions for HOME. She can visit his workroom in 3024 and return, but the machine is old, and Alex asked her not to travel.

She looks at the photograph again. Then she checks the power: still green. She decides to make a short trip, although she knows that Alex will worry. At ten o'clock, she selects HOME, closes the door, and presses GO.

White light fills the cabin. When it disappears, the alley is gone. Through the window, Olga sees the workroom from the photograph. The screen reads June 27,

3024.

She opens the door. A man carrying a tool bag stops in the corridor and stares at her. His bag slips from his hand, scattering tools across the floor.

“I'm looking for information about Alex,” Olga says.

“You know Alex?”

“He's safe. He's with people who are helping him.”

The man introduces himself as Pavel, Alex's colleague. Two other men come into the corridor, followed by several more. They ask questions at the same time and move closer. Olga steps back toward the machine.

“Give her some room,” Pavel says. “She can't answer everyone at once.”

Through the corridor window, Olga sees roads without traffic and apartment buildings with dark windows. No children are playing outside. Pavel tells her about the disappearance of the women and girls twenty years earlier. Now she understands why Alex listened so carefully to the noise in her café.

She asks Pavel to let the people at work know that Alex is alive. Then an alarm sounds inside the machine. The power light changes from green to red, and Olga hurries back to the machine.

The screen shows LOW POWER. There is enough energy for one return, but she cannot choose a new arrival time. RETURN offers the last saved moment: June 27, 2024, 8:00 p.m.

“That's before dinner,” she whispers.

She finally understands the blue dress, the loose hair, and the woman at the restaurant. She is going to be that woman. Olga presses RETURN and holds the recorder against her chest. The cabin shakes, and her hair falls loose. Beside her, a loose panel leaves a dark mark on her sleeve.

### 2 Грамматика

### Can и cannot

После can и cannot используется начальная форма глагола без to и без окончания -s: she can return; she cannot choose. Cannot можно сократить до can't. Выберите правильный вариант в скобках.

1. Olga can (see / sees) the workroom.

2. Pavel can (help / to help) her.

3. The men cannot (understand / understands) everything at once.

4. Olga cannot (choose / chose) a new arrival time.

5. The machine can (return / returns) to the saved moment.

### 3 Понимание текста

2. Who is Pavel?

4. Why can't Olga return at a later time that evening?
