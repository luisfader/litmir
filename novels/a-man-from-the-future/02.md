### Moscow · 3024

Alex lives in Moscow in the year 3024. He is twenty-nine, and he repairs computers in a large gray building. The computers can answer almost any question. But they cannot tell him how to stop feeling lonely.

Twenty years ago, all the women and girls in his world disappeared. No one knows why. Alex remembers his mother singing in the kitchen, but he cannot remember the whole song. There are no children now, and the city grows quieter every year.

One afternoon, his manager asks him to clean an old workroom. Behind some empty boxes, Alex finds a red machine with a door and a small window. Dust covers its screen. Above the door, he reads two words: TIME MACHINE.

At first, Alex laughs. Then he finds a notebook in a drawer beside the machine. It contains instructions, old travel records, and pictures of the same street in different years. The machine is real, and someone used it before.

Alex takes the notebook home. He reads it twice before he goes to bed, but he does not sleep much. He thinks about busy streets, music, and people sitting together in cafés. Somewhere in the past, that world still exists.

The next morning, he returns with a small backpack. He cleans the machine, checks its power unit, and reads the instructions again. The green button saves a place, a date, and a time. HOME takes the traveler to this workroom in 3024, keeping the same month, day, and time. RETURN takes the traveler to the last saved moment.

One page has a warning: returning to a time before you leave can create a meeting with your earlier self. Alex reads the sentence slowly. He understands the words, but the idea makes his head hurt.

“One of me is enough,” he says to the empty room.

He saves the workroom as HOME, then chooses Moscow, June 20, 2024, at nine in the morning. The screen shows a small alley near Tverskaya Street. He steps inside, closes the door, and presses GO.

The floor shakes. White light fills the window, and Alex holds the side of his seat. A few seconds later, the noise stops. The screen reads 2024.

Outside, a delivery driver is arguing with a bicycle rider. A woman walks past with a child, and a dog pulls its owner toward a tree. Alex stands in the doorway, smiling at all of them. Even the argument sounds wonderful.

He locks the machine and puts the key in his pocket. It looks like a large metal cabinet beside the other boxes in the alley. Then he walks toward the sound of traffic, carrying his backpack and a plan that suddenly seems very simple.

He wants to meet a woman, fall in love, and bring her home with him. He does not yet think about whether she wants the same thing.

1. Alex _______ (live) in Moscow.

2. He _______ (repair) computers.

3. The machine _______ (have) a small window.

4. The lights _______ (flash) when the machine starts.

5. Alex _______ (go) into the past.
