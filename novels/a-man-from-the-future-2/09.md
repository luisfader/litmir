### Nina's Café · July 5, 2025

The final exam was simple. Each man had to go on a practice date in Nina's café with a volunteer. Svetlana sat in the corner with a clipboard and a whistle. If a man made a serious mistake, she blew the whistle, and he failed.

“If you pass,” Olga told them on the morning of the exam, “you'll get a certificate. If you fail, you'll go home tomorrow. Any questions?”

Gleb raised his hand. “If I'm nervous, will I fail?”

“No. If you're nervous, you'll be normal.”

The first student was a man called Arkady. He sat down, looked at his date, and said, “Hello. You are a woman. Statistically, you are excellent.”

*Whistle.*

“If you start with statistics,” Svetlana said, “you'll go home with statistics.”

The second man brought a present. It was a bunch of flowers, and he didn't eat a single petal. Everyone clapped. Then he took the flowers back, because “in 3024, we return items after demonstration”.

*Whistle.*

The third man did everything correctly. He said hello. He asked his date about her day. He listened to the answer. Then he took out a measuring tape and asked if he could measure her for the time machine seat.

*Whistle.* Svetlana didn't even look up.

By lunchtime, forty men had failed. Nina served soup to the losers, who were sitting on the steps outside, looking at the ground.

“If it goes on like this,” Alex said quietly to Olga, “nobody will pass.”

“Wait,” said Olga. “It's Gleb's turn.”

Gleb walked to the table. He was carrying his notebook, and his hands were shaking. His date was a librarian called Vera. She was about thirty, with glasses and a serious face.

Gleb sat down. He opened his mouth. He closed it. He opened the notebook, looked at it, and closed it again.

“I'm sorry,” he said finally. “I prepared forty-two questions. But now I don't remember any of them.”

Svetlana lifted her whistle.

“If I can't remember my questions,” Gleb continued quickly, “can I ask what you're reading?”

He pointed at the book in Vera's bag. Vera looked surprised.

“It's a novel about a man who travels in time,” she said.

Gleb's eyes became enormous. “Does he find love?”

“I don't know yet. I'm in the middle.”

“If he finds love,” said Gleb seriously, “will you tell me?”

Vera smiled for the first time. “If you come to the library on Tuesday, I'll tell you.”

Svetlana lowered the whistle very slowly.

Then it was Boris's turn. The whole café held its breath. Boris was the biggest, loudest, and hungriest student in the course. In two weeks he broke three chairs, a door, and the school piano.

His date was — and nobody knew who organized this — Svetlana herself. She gave the clipboard to Nina and sat down across from him.

Boris looked at her. Svetlana looked at Boris.

“Svetlana Petrovna,” he said.

“Boris.”

“If I say something stupid, will you blow the whistle?”

“Of course.”

Boris nodded. He put his enormous hands on the table, carefully, as if the table were made of glass.

“Two weeks ago,” he said, “I came to take a woman home. Like a box. I didn't know anything. Now I know one thing.” He took a deep breath. “If somebody is shouting at you every day, for two weeks, from seven in the morning, it doesn't mean she doesn't like you. It means she's trying.”

Svetlana didn't move.

“Thank you for trying,” said Boris. “That's all.”

The café was so quiet that everyone heard the coffee machine. Then Svetlana did something nobody had ever seen.

She blushed.

“If you tell anybody about this,” she said, “I'll send you back to 3024 personally.”

“I won't tell anybody,” Boris promised.

Fedya, who was watching from the door with Masha, already had his phone out.

### 2 Грамматика

### First Conditional

Первый тип условных предложений описывает реальную ситуацию в будущем: If + Present Simple, will + глагол. If you pass, you'll get a certificate. If you fail, you'll go home. После if не ставится will. Поставьте глаголы в нужную форму.

1. If you _______ (pass), you _______ (get) a certificate.

2. If Gleb _______ (be) nervous, he _______ (not / fail).

3. If you _______ (start) with statistics, you _______ (go) home.

4. If Gleb _______ (come) to the library on Tuesday, Vera _______ (tell) him the ending.

5. If Boris _______ (say) something stupid, Svetlana _______ (blow) the whistle.

6. What _______ you _______ (do) if you _______ (fail) the exam?

2. Why did the third man fail?

3. What did Gleb ask Vera?

4. Who was Boris's date?

5. What did Boris understand about Svetlana?
