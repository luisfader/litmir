### Moscow · June 2025

A year passed, and Alex's life changed a lot.

In September, he married Olga in a small registry office. Nina cried during the whole ceremony. Svetlana came too, and she congratulated them so loudly that a pigeon fell off the window. After the wedding, they didn't take a time machine. They took a train to Saint Petersburg, and Alex looked out of the window for six hours.

“Everything moves so slowly,” he said.

“That's called a journey,” Olga answered. “People enjoy it.”

In winter, Alex found a better job. He repaired computers in a big office on Tverskaya Street, and his colleagues liked him. He was a little strange. He didn't understand jokes about TV shows, and once he tried to pay for lunch with his wrist. But he fixed every computer in two minutes, so nobody complained.

Olga drew pictures for children's books. Her reading club in Nina's café grew, and every Thursday twenty people came to talk about novels. Alex made the coffee. At first, his coffee was terrible. By spring, it was only bad. By summer, people actually drank it.

Life was normal. Alex loved normal. In 3024, nothing was ever normal, because nothing ever happened.

On the morning of June 20, 2025, exactly one year after his first journey, Alex woke up early. He made breakfast, burned the toast, and opened the window. It was a beautiful day. Birds were singing. A neighbor was walking her dog.

Then his phone rang. It was Nina.

“Alex,” she said. “Come to the café. Now.”

“What happened?”

“A man came in at seven o'clock. He ordered a coffee, then he looked at me and asked me to marry him.”

Alex laughed. “Maybe he liked your coffee.”

“He asked me before he tasted it. Then he asked the waitress. Then he asked a woman at the bus stop. He's wearing a silver suit, Alex. A silver suit with a number on it.”

Alex stopped laughing. He knew those suits. In 3024, every worker wore one. His own suit was in a box under the bed.

“What number?” he asked quietly.

“Three thousand and something. Why?”

Alex didn't answer. He was looking out of the window. Down in the street, a second man in a silver suit was standing in front of a flower shop. He picked up a rose, smelled it, and then — very seriously — took a bite.

Olga came into the kitchen in her pajamas. She saw Alex's face and stopped.

“What's wrong?”

“I think,” Alex said slowly, “we have visitors.”

Olga looked out of the window. The man was now eating a second rose. The shop assistant was shouting at him, and he was smiling at her with petals in his teeth.

“Alex,” Olga said. “Is that…”

“A man from the future,” Alex said. “Like me. Only worse.”

“Worse than you on your first day?” Olga raised her eyebrows. “You tried to pay with your wrist.”

“He's eating flowers, Olga.”

Olga thought about it.

“Okay,” she said. “Worse.”

### 2 Грамматика

### Past Simple

Past Simple описывает законченные действия в прошлом. Правильные глаголы получают окончание -ed: work → worked. Неправильные глаголы нужно запомнить: go → went, take → took, come → came. Отрицание и вопрос строятся с did: He didn't take a time machine. Did he find a job? Поставьте глаголы в скобках в Past Simple.

1. Alex _______ (marry) Olga in September.

2. Svetlana _______ (come) to the wedding.

3. They _______ (take) a train to Saint Petersburg.

4. Alex _______ (not / understand) jokes about TV shows.

5. A man _______ (ask) Nina to marry him.

6. _______ the man _______ (taste) the coffee first?
