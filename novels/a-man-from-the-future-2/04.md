### Moscow · June 20, 2025 · 11:30

Fedya had a small recorder in his pocket. It looked exactly like the recorder that Alex brought to 2024 a year ago.

“Pavel gave it to me,” Fedya said. “He told me: ‘If you find Alex, give him this. And please, Fedya, don't eat it.’”

“Why would you eat it?” Olga asked.

Fedya looked at the sugar bowl and didn't answer.

Alex pressed the button. The screen lit up, and Pavel's tired face appeared. He was sitting in the old workroom. Behind him, somebody was hitting a machine with a hammer.

“Alex,” Pavel said. “If you're watching this, it's too late. I'm sorry. I'm really sorry. Let me explain.”

Everyone in the café came closer. Even Boris stopped licking the sugar spoon.

“After you left, I kept your notebook,” Pavel said. “I wanted to protect it. I put it in a safe place — in my desk. Then, three months ago, I was eating lunch when the manager walked in. He saw the notebook. He read it. And he read the part about Olga.”

On the screen, Pavel rubbed his face.

“While I was trying to take it back, he was already photographing every page. By the evening, the whole city was reading it. Your love story became the most popular text in 3024. Men were reading it on the metro. They were reading it at work. One man was reading it to his plants.”

“That was me,” Gleb said proudly.

“Then the engineers studied the machine,” Pavel continued. “They built a copy. Then a hundred copies. Then three thousand. They called it ‘Operation Wife’. I tried to stop them. I told them that a woman is a person, not a product. They didn't understand. They think the notebook is an instruction manual. Step one: travel. Step two: buy coffee. Step three: get a wife.”

Olga slowly turned to Alex.

“Your notebook,” she said, “is an instruction manual?”

“It was a diary!”

“You wrote about me in a diary that is now the most popular text in the future?”

Alex opened his mouth and closed it again.

On the screen, there was a loud crash behind Pavel.

“They're leaving today,” he said quickly. “All of them. They all chose the same date — your date, June 20. Please, Alex. You're the only one who knows both worlds. Help them. Or help the women. Or help everyone. I don't know. Good luck. And… say hello to Olga. Tell her I'm sorry about the diary.”

The screen went dark.

For a long moment, nobody spoke. Outside, there was another *POP* in the alley, and somebody said, “Is this 2025?”

Then Svetlana opened the café door. She was holding a man in a silver suit by the ear.

“This one,” she announced, “tried to put me in a shopping cart. Whose is he?”

“Nobody's,” said Alex. “Everybody's. It's complicated.”

Svetlana looked around the café: at Boris with sugar in his beard, at Gleb with his notebook, at Fedya, who was smiling at her as if she were the sun.

“Alex,” she said. “Last year you followed me down the street and asked me strange questions. I forgave you because you were only one man.” She pointed outside, where the men were now climbing a lamp post to look at the city. “How many are there?”

“About three thousand,” Alex said.

Svetlana let go of the ear.

“Then,” she said, “we need a plan.”

### 2 Грамматика

### Past Continuous и Past Simple

Past Continuous (was / were + -ing) описывает длительное действие в прошлом. Past Simple — короткое действие, которое его прервало: I was eating lunch when the manager walked in. Со словом while обычно используется Past Continuous. Выберите правильную форму.

1. Pavel (ate / was eating) lunch when the manager walked in.

2. While Pavel (tried / was trying) to take the notebook back, the manager photographed it.

3. Men (read / were reading) the story on the metro when the engineers built the first copy.

4. Svetlana (walked / was walking) to the café when a man tried to put her in a cart.

5. When Svetlana (opened / was opening) the door, everyone looked at her.
