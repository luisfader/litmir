### Moscow · June 20, 2025 · 9:00

When Alex and Olga arrived at the café, the street looked like a strange festival.

Men in silver suits were standing everywhere. Some were looking at cars. Some were touching trees, as if they were checking that the trees were real. One man was trying to talk to a parking meter. Another was following a group of tourists and politely asking each woman: “Excuse me. Are you available for marriage?”

“Why is he saying it like that?” Olga whispered.

“In 3024, that's how you order something,” Alex said. “‘Is this item available?’ He's using the only polite sentence he knows.”

Inside the café, Nina was standing behind the counter with a rolling pin. In front of her, three men were sitting at a table for two. They were eating sugar from the sugar bowl with their fingers.

“Good morning,” said the biggest man. He was enormous, with a red beard and hands like shovels. “My name is Boris. Number 3417. I am looking for a wife. You are a woman. Excellent. Please come with me to the future.”

“I'm sixty-two,” Nina said.

“Is that a problem?” Boris asked. “I am not choosing. I am collecting.”

Nina lifted the rolling pin.

“Alex,” she said without turning around. “Explain to your friends that I am not a souvenir.”

Alex sat down at the table. The three men looked at him with interest.

“You're wearing normal clothes,” said the second man. He was thin and nervous and had a notebook in his hand. “I'm Gleb. 3502. Why are you dressed like a local?”

“Because I live here,” Alex said. “How did you get here?”

The third man, young and round-faced, smiled happily. He had sugar on his nose.

“I'm Fedya,” he said. “We came in the machines. Everybody's coming. Haven't you heard? You're famous, Alex. You're the man who found a woman.”

Olga, who was standing by the door, crossed her arms.

“He's the man who *found* a woman?”

“Yes,” said Fedya. “Like finding a coin in the street. Everybody's talking about it.”

“Fedya,” Alex said quickly, “that isn't what—”

But at that moment, the window shook. Outside, a white light was flashing in the alley next to the café. Then another one. And another. Each flash came with a loud *POP*, and after each pop, a new man in a silver suit walked out of the alley, looked around, and said, “Is this 2025?”

Olga took out her phone and opened the news. Her face changed.

“Alex. They're not only here. They're arriving all over Moscow. Red Square. The metro. The zoo. Someone is asking a bronze statue if it's available for marriage.”

“A statue?”

“It's a female statue,” Gleb explained, writing in his notebook. “We have very limited information about women.”

Nina put down the rolling pin and poured herself a large glass of water.

“How many of you are there?” she asked.

Boris thought about it. He counted on his enormous fingers, then gave up.

“All of us,” he said.

### 2 Грамматика

### Present Continuous

Present Continuous описывает действия, которые происходят прямо сейчас: am / is / are + глагол с -ing. Men are standing everywhere. Is he eating sugar? They aren't listening. Поставьте глаголы в скобках в Present Continuous.

1. Some men _______ (look) at cars.

2. One man _______ (try) to talk to a parking meter.

3. Nina _______ (stand) behind the counter.

4. The men _______ (eat) sugar with their fingers.

5. Gleb _______ (write) in his notebook.

6. _______ they _______ (arrive) all over Moscow? — Yes, they are.
