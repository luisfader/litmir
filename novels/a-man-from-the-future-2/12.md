### Module 1. First Words

| Don't say | Say |
| You are a woman. Excellent. | Hello! Nice to meet you. |
| Are you available for marriage? | Would you like to have a coffee with me? |
| Statistically, you are excellent. | I'm really glad we met. |
| Please come with me to the future. | Can I see you again? |

### Module 2. Compliments

**Good:**

- You have a lovely smile.
- I like talking to you.
- That's a really interesting idea.
- You look great today.
- When you laughed, I forgot about my pancake.

**Bad (whistle!):**

- You have very strong bones.
- You remind me of my favorite machine.
- Your face has a good number of eyes.

### Module 3. Polite Phrases

| Situation | Phrase |
| You want something | Could I have…, please? |
| Somebody helps you | Thank you very much. That's very kind of you. |
| You make a mistake | I'm sorry. My mistake. |
| You don't understand | Sorry, could you say that again? |
| You want to leave | It was lovely to see you. I have to go now. |
| Somebody gives you flowers | Oh, they're beautiful! I'll put them in water. |

### Module 4. When She Says…

| She says | It means | You should |
| Yes. | Yes. | Smile. Don't shout “EXCELLENT”. |
| No. | No. | Say “Okay, no problem.” Go home. |
| Maybe. | Maybe. | Wait. Don't ask again in five minutes. |
| I'm busy. | She is busy. Or she means “no”. | Don't ask her sister. |
| Let's be friends. | Let's be friends. | Be a good friend. That's a lot. |
