### Moscow City Hall · June 20, 2025 · 15:00

By the afternoon, the city was in chaos.

The men from the future were everywhere. They were faster than the police and louder than the traffic. They were hungrier than teenagers. In one supermarket, forty men ate all the bananas in twenty minutes — with the skins. In the metro, a group of men tried to propose to the voice that announced the stations. The voice didn't answer, and the men were very disappointed.

At three o'clock, the mayor called an emergency meeting. The biggest room in City Hall was full of police officers, doctors, scientists, and journalists. Everybody was talking at the same time.

“Who are they?”

“Where are they from?”

“Why are they eating the furniture?”

“Only one of them ate the furniture,” said a police officer. “And it was a very small chair.”

The mayor hit the table with his hand.

“Quiet! We need an expert. Is there anybody in this city who understands these men?”

The room went silent. Then, at the back, Olga pushed Alex forward.

“He does,” she said. “He was one of them.”

Three hundred people turned to look at Alex. He walked slowly to the front and stood next to the mayor. His hands were shaking a little.

“Good afternoon,” he said. “My name is Alex. I'm from 3024. A year ago, I was the first man who came here. I was the worst at everything. Now I'm only bad at some things.”

A few people laughed. That helped.

“You have to understand,” Alex continued. “These men are not dangerous. They're just… the most confused people in the world. In 3024, there are no women. There haven't been women for twenty-one years. These men grew up with machines, rules, and other men. They don't know how to talk to women. They don't know how to talk about feelings. For them, a conversation is shorter than a shopping list.”

“Then why are they so loud?” asked a journalist.

“Because in 3024, it's quieter than a library. Here, everything is louder and brighter and more exciting than at home. They're like children at a birthday party.”

“Why do they eat flowers?” asked a doctor.

“Because flowers look more delicious than our food. Our food in 3024 is gray. It's the most boring food in history.”

“Why do they think every woman wants to marry them?”

Alex sighed. “Because they read my diary. And my diary has a happy ending.”

Somebody at the back laughed again. Olga didn't.

The mayor looked at Alex for a long time.

“So,” he said. “What do they want?”

“Love,” said Alex. “But they think it's easier than it is. They think love is like buying a ticket. You go to the window, you pay, you get it.”

“And what is love really like?”

Alex looked at Olga. She was standing at the back with her arms crossed, but she was almost smiling.

“Harder than repairing a time machine,” he said. “And much better.”

The mayor nodded slowly. Then he pointed at Alex.

“Congratulations,” he said. “You are now the official expert on men from the future. You have one day to make a plan.”

“One day? For three thousand men?”

“You did it with one,” said the mayor. “Think of it as a bigger version.”

### Сравнительная и превосходная степень

Короткие прилагательные: loud → louder → the loudest; big → bigger → the biggest; easy → easier → the easiest. Длинные: more exciting → the most exciting. Исключения: good → better → the best; bad → worse → the worst. Поставьте прилагательные в нужную форму.

1. The men were _______ (fast) than the police.

2. Here everything is _______ (bright) than in 3024.

3. They are the _______ (confused) people in the world.

4. Our food in 3024 is the _______ (boring) food in history.

5. Love is _______ (hard) than repairing a time machine.

6. A year ago, Alex was the _______ (bad) at everything.
