SINEA. — The hotel "Dark Sun" is an old building near the port. For years, it had a simple rule: no document, no room. And a second, less famous rule: unless you pay a little extra.

Last week, the court explained how the second rule worked. The receptionist was sentenced to two years in prison.

## The business model

According to the two policemen who confessed in court, the system was simple. Every night, they stopped immigrants in the street and asked for their papers. People with no papers paid them money. Then the policemen sent the immigrants to the hotel "Dark Sun", where the receptionist asked for a document, did not get one, and found "a solution for the problem". In exchange, he gave the policemen half of his bribes.

Economists call this a closed circuit. The judge called it corruption.

## The diamond under the road

The same two policemen also stole the famous turquoise diamond from the city museum. They had keys. They did not break anything. They turned off the alarm, opened the glass box and took the diamond. Then they drove one block and put it in a hole under a sign: ROAD WORK – NEW ASPHALT TOMORROW.

Nobody was going to find it. Ever. Then a road worker, an immigrant with no papers, dug a hole in that road and found it. He gave it back.

The man who paid the policemen was the Mayor himself. On the radio, before the election, he told the city: "We believe the thief is an immigrant." It was, he later explained to his daughter, "politics". People need a simple story before an election.

## The sentences

- **Martin**, former Mayor of Sinea: ten years in prison for slavery, for the theft of the diamond and for corruption.
- **The two policemen**: four years.
- **The receptionist** of the hotel "Dark Sun": two years.
- **Leo**, the hacker who made false papers: no prison, but two hundred hours of community work teaching computers to children.

## A review

*The Sinea Herald* asked the immigrant who found the diamond what he remembers about the hotel. "It was very small," he said. "And the woman who sent me there did not know. She said sorry."

That woman is now his fiancée. The hotel is now looking for a new receptionist. Documents required.
