MOSCOW, 3024. — Twenty years ago, all the women and girls in the world disappeared. Today, the official explanation is the same as on the first day: no one knows why.

There are no children now. Most meals come from food machines. Life is gray, quiet and very well organized. The future, it seems, works perfectly. It just does not taste like anything.

## A citizen leaves for 2024

Last month a young man named Alex was asked by his manager to clean an old workroom. Behind some empty boxes, he found a red machine with a door and a small window. In a drawer there was a notebook with instructions, old travel records and pictures of the same street in different years.

At first, Alex laughed. Then he read the instructions again. According to the notebook, the green button saves a place, a date and a time. HOME brings you back.

Alex chose Moscow, June 20, 2024, nine in the morning.

## The pastry problem

Our sources in 2024 report that his first adventure was a cup of coffee. In a small café, a young woman called Olga put a pastry on a plate and told him the price. Alex held his finger above the payment machine. Nothing happened. He tried again.

When he finally ate, the first bite left him silent. Experts in 3024 have studied this reaction and call it "taste". It is not available from food machines.

## Documents, please

The trip has created a legal problem. In 2024, Alex has no documents. His little chip, which in 3024 opens every door, cannot replace a passport. Olga, who works in her aunt Nina's café, was the first to ask the practical question: what will he do about it?

At the time of writing, Alex was still thinking. According to people close to him, he was also thinking about Olga. A lot.

## What the future says

*The Sinea Herald* asked a resident of 3024 for a comment. He said he remembers his mother singing in the kitchen, but he cannot remember the whole song.

We do not have a joke for that.
